How much to trust this

Not provenStrongest gap: N1 vs P6, t = 1.89. V4 vs N1, t = 0.33. Proof needs about 2.5.
Data already seenThe 2024–26 periods were looked at while building these models. Only data from 9 Oct 2026 onward is truly new.
Costs are modelled60% maker fills are assumed. V4 trades 70× a year, so if real costs are 2×, most of its profit lead disappears.
Paper before moneyP6 failed its last clean test. Every model needs months of positive paper results after costs first.
